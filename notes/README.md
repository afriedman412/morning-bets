# notes/

Loose notes. **None of this is authority.**

`AF_PLAN.md` is what the project is for and `CLAUDE.md` is the working
agreement; `RESUME.md` and `NOTES-context-layer.md` are the log,
`TODO.md` and `PLAN-baseball-logic.md` are the backlog. Where anything here
disagrees with one of those, the other one is right.

These lived at the repo root, where four untitled files sat beside the six
documents that do bind. Contents are unchanged from where they were written.

- **the-plan.md** — the original one-paragraph statement of the project,
  before `AF_PLAN.md`. Superseded, kept because it is where the standing
  argument for prioritising pitching over hitting was first written down.
- **sim-open-questions.md** — a running list of things about the simulator
  that have been asked and not yet settled. Working material; items graduate
  out of here into `PLAN-baseball-logic.md` or `TODO.md`.
- **resume-framing.md** — how to present the original pipeline as a
  portfolio project. Nothing to do with the simulator.
- **bullpen-pitch-counts-2026-08-28.txt** — a RotoWire paste of one day's
  bullpen pitch counts, the raw material behind the bullpen-availability
  work. Superseded by `hook_penstate.json`, which is generated.
