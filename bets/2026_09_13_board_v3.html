What the numbers carry

Comparing to a sportsbook

Kalshi’s two sides are one contract, so they mirror exactly. A book’s two sides carry its hold, so de-vig before comparing: an under at −124 alone implies 55.4%, but with the over near +103 on the same book its true under is about 53%.

Strikeouts

Usable as printed from 4.5 to 7.5. At 8.5 and up the tail is light and two points are already added to those overs. Against a closing price the K model adds nothing — its whole measured value is being early.

Totals

July and August ran roughly 0.15 to 0.20 runs light a side; September is unmeasured. The engine over-weights three-to-six run games, so a low-total under lean is bias, not a read.

Not here at all
